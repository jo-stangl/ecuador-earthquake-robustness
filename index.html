For each main-text exhibit of Resilience to natural disasters and shock propagation in firm-level production networks (the 2016 Pedernales earthquake, Ecuador), this page shows the robustness checks reported in the Supplementary Information (SI). Every number is an aggregate result behind an SI figure or table (regression coefficients and their standard errors; for Fig. 2, shares of firms). The page holds no firm-level data.

How to read it. Pick a tab. The figure shows the main-text estimate, the reference. The buttons below the tab introduction are the checks, grouped by the threat to the result each one addresses, most serious first. Clicking a check overlays it (open circles with their 95% intervals). The box above the figure says what the check changes and which estimate it is compared with; the equation highlights the changed terms. The table Does the headline survive? lists, for every check, how far it moves a few headline coefficients, in standard errors of the estimate it is compared with: about 2 means the check lands at the edge of that estimate's 95% interval. Cells are shaded from 1 standard error (light) and 2 (dark).

τ
months from the earthquake (April 2016 = 0). The reference window is the set of pre-event months effects are measured against: τ = -18 to -7 for direct effects, -12 to -1 for propagation.
MMI
Modified Mercalli Intensity, the shaking severity of the firm's parish (USGS ShakeMap). Bins: below 5 (control), [5,6), [6,7), [7,7.5), [7.5,8), ≥ 8.
Log points
outcomes are log(1 + monthly purchases or sales); a coefficient β is a 100 (eβ - 1) percent change (-0.30 is about -26%).
Fixed effects (FE)
firm and year-month in the main specifications; "sector / province / region × month" compares firms only within that group in the same month.
SE, 95% CI
standard error and 95% confidence interval, clustered by parish (direct effects) or by firm (propagation).
Samples
main: all firms, including personas naturales (individuals registered as taxpayers, not formal firms); robustness: formal firms only (personas naturales excluded on both sides of each transaction).
Exposure controls
a firm's pre-event purchases from and sales to parishes at MMI ≥ 7, weighted by their MMI (SI Eq. S1); they guard against control firms being hit through their suppliers and customers.
Acute / recovery window
τ = 0-1 / τ = 2-12 (sector results).
Core / outer disaster zone
parishes at MMI ≥ 7.5 / firms in parishes below MMI 7; the band [7,7.5) is a buffer that belongs to neither.
Supply / demand channel
the core-zone firm is the outer-zone firm's supplier (outcome: its purchases) / customer (outcome: its sales).
Network distance
number of substantial trading links (≥ 2% of the firm's pre-event purchases or sales) to the nearest core-zone firm.
Trade exposure
share of a firm's pre-event purchases from (or sales to) the core disaster zone.
Pass-through elasticity ρ
how strongly a contraction at a firm's suppliers or customers is passed on to the firm, estimated by two-stage least squares (2SLS) with trade exposure as instrument; a first-stage F below 10 marks a weak instrument.
Wild-cluster bootstrap
inference that resamples whole parishes, so that it stays reliable when few parishes are treated (nine at MMI ≥ 8).
HonestDiD sensitivity bounds
how large a violation of parallel trends after the event an effect survives, relative to the largest pre-event deviation.
ISIC, PPML
international industry classification (sections); Poisson pseudo-maximum likelihood, which weights firms by their value.
